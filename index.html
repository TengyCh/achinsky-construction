The Foundation

Building your vision, ensuring quality

Established in 2006 in Mongolia, Achinsky LLC has rapidly evolved into a prominent name in the construction industry, primarily serving the Mongolian market. This esteemed company, wholly owned by its General Director, Mr. Chinbat Amar, has carved out a niche in architectural design, interior and exterior design, and construction.

Since its inception, Achinsky LLC has been synonymous with high-quality project delivery, maintaining robust relationships with its customers. The Foreign Affiliate has also established a reliable partnership status with various financial institutions, both banking and non-banking.
